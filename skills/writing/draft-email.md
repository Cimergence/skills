# Draft Email

**Category:** Writing
**Tags:** email, communication, professional, drafting

## Description

Ask Claude to draft a professional email from a short brief. Saves time when you know what you want to say but need help structuring and wording it clearly.

## Prompt Template

```
Draft a professional email with the following details:

- To: [RECIPIENT / RECIPIENT'S ROLE]
- From: [YOUR NAME / ROLE]
- Purpose: [WHAT THE EMAIL NEEDS TO ACHIEVE]
- Key points to include:
  - [POINT 1]
  - [POINT 2]
  - [POINT 3]
- Tone: [formal / semi-formal / friendly but professional]
- Length: [brief (~100 words) / standard (~200 words) / detailed (~300 words)]

Do not add information beyond what is listed above.
```

## Example

**Input:**
> Draft a professional email with the following details:
> - To: Project stakeholders
> - From: Engineering lead
> - Purpose: Inform them of a one-week delay to the product launch
> - Key points: The delay is due to a critical bug found in QA; the team is working on a fix; new launch date is April 14th; no other scope changes
> - Tone: semi-formal
> - Length: brief (~100 words)

**Output:**
> Subject: Product Launch Update — Revised Date of April 14th
>
> Hi all,
>
> I wanted to give you a quick update on our upcoming launch. During final QA testing, the team identified a critical bug that we need to resolve before going live. To ensure we ship a stable product, we are moving the launch date to **April 14th** — one week from the original schedule.
>
> There are no other changes to scope or deliverables. The team is actively working on the fix and we remain confident in the new date.
>
> Thank you for your understanding. Please reach out if you have any questions.
>
> Best,
> [Name]

## Notes

- Including a subject line in the "Key points" section or asking Claude to suggest one both work well.
- If you receive a draft you want to adjust, follow up with: "Make it shorter" / "Make the tone warmer" / "Add a specific ask at the end".
