# Summarise Document

**Category:** Writing
**Tags:** summarisation, reading, research, notes

## Description

Ask Claude to produce a concise, structured summary of any document — article, report, paper, or transcript. Great for quickly extracting the key points from long-form content.

## Prompt Template

```
Summarise the following [DOCUMENT TYPE: article / research paper / meeting transcript / report] in [LENGTH: 3 bullet points / a short paragraph / an executive summary of ~200 words].

Highlight:
- The main argument or purpose
- Key findings or points
- Any conclusions or recommended actions

[PASTE DOCUMENT TEXT HERE]
```

## Example

**Input:**
> Summarise the following article in 3 bullet points. Highlight the main argument, key findings, and conclusions.
>
> [Article about sleep research pasted here]

**Output:**
> - **Main argument:** Chronic sleep deprivation of even one to two hours per night significantly impairs cognitive performance, comparable to going without sleep for 24 hours.
> - **Key finding:** Adults consistently underestimate their own impairment — subjects who slept 6 hours per night rated themselves as only "slightly sleepy" while performing at the level of someone who had been awake for 24 hours.
> - **Conclusion:** The study recommends that public health guidelines reinforce 7–9 hours as a minimum, and that workplaces should reconsider norms that reward long hours over rest.

## Notes

- For very long documents, paste sections separately and ask for a combined summary at the end.
- You can ask Claude to preserve specific details (e.g., "keep all numerical statistics in the summary").
- Pair with [Rewrite for Audience](rewrite-for-audience.md) to adapt the summary for a specific reader.
